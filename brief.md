# ONS Well-being Survey: Project Brief

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) is the largest independent producer of official statistics in the UK. The ONS wants to test a short online version of its personal well-being questions with a group of recruited participants. In your team, you will make a web app that shows this short survey. Participants must sign in, complete the survey one time, and save their answers. The app is a standalone system. It doesn't connect to other ONS systems. The app must use the ONS design theme, so that it looks and works like other ONS services.

This brief doesn't contain all the requirements. Your first task is to find them. Two stakeholder personas will answer your questions: Alice, the Security and Platform Lead, and Bob, the Survey Product Owner. You need to identify suitable questions to ask and record what they tell you as user stories with acceptance criteria.

## Provided materials and resources

- A starter template repository on GitHub. It contains a small Flask app with the ONS Design System page layout and one test.
- In 15 minutes, the teams can ask the the two stakeholders questions in an open forum.
- After the open forum you may ask any of the facilitators additional clarifying questions.

## Deliverables

- Your GitHub repository, with a README that tells a new developer how to run and test the app.
- Your requirements, as user stories with acceptance criteria.
- A working demo of the app on your computer or in GitHub Codespaces.
- A passing automated build in GitHub Actions.
- An architecture diagram and a cost estimate.
- A 10 minute presentation about your solution.
